# Layer 6C₄: confirmation test for C3 on the Decker worldwide panel

**Status: DRAFT, not frozen, not run.** To be frozen with its SHA-256 in `output/layer6c4/PROTOCOL_LOCK_6C4.json` before any Decker support score, ancestry coordinate or acceptance decision is computed. Items marked **[implement]** must exist and be hashed before the lock.

## What this is for

C3 is the project's only positive external finding, and it is classed **POST_HOC_SUPPORTED**, not confirmed: it was generated by a diagnosis run *after* the frozen 6C₂ endpoint had already failed. Its claim:

> The k-NN empirical support region refuses a population that the min–max ancestry bounds it replaced would have accepted.

Confirmation requires a second out-of-reference population, scored under a rule fixed before its support scores are seen. This is that test.

**Why Decker can serve.** The 6C₃ coverage clause stopped the arm before anything was computed, so **no Decker support score or ancestry coordinate has ever been produced**. The data are on disk, both files MD5-verified, and unseen in the only sense that matters. The pre-registered stop preserved them as a clean confirmation set.

## Data

Decker et al. 2014, Dryad `10.5061/dryad.th092` (CC0): **1,543 animals, 133 breed labels, Illumina BovineSNP50 (43,043 markers), UMD3.1**, breed labels in `ww_cattle_dryad_20Sept2013.pedind`. Genotypes are Illumina A/B coded (`1` = A allele, `2` = B allele, per the Dryad README).

43 breeds hold exactly 20 animals, 70 hold at least 10. This is the arm's main advantage over 6C₂: not one out-of-reference population but dozens, so the endpoint is a rule applied across breeds rather than a claim about a single cohort.

## Harmonisation, and the QA gate that precedes any endpoint

- **Coordinates.** UMD3.1 → ARS-UCD1.2 with the stored UCSC chain (`src/real_data/liftover.py`), then position matching against the 20,087-marker panel. Position matching gave **2,891** markers; the count after allele checking is not yet known and is computed at the prepare stage.
- **Alleles.** Decker gives Illumina A/B codes, and `allele_lookup_f250.csv` records which Illumina allele Florida counted (`florida_counted_code`). Dosage of the Florida counted allele is therefore read directly from the A/B codes, with **no strand or nucleotide inference**. This assumes the two manifests assign A/B consistently per marker, which the gate below tests rather than assumes.
- **Coverage requirement, fixed here:** at least **2,000 usable markers** after allele checking. Justification is the calibration curve in `docs/COVERAGE_CALIBRATION.md`: out-of-domain refusal is 0.970 at 2,000 markers and 0.992 at 2,891. Below 2,000 the rule degrades visibly (0.898 at 1,000) and the arm stops. **If the usable count falls between 2,000 and 2,891, the arm proceeds with the label that its expected out-of-domain refusal is about 0.97 rather than 0.99.** This threshold is set before the usable count is known.
- **A/B consistency gate [implement].** Allele frequencies are compared between Decker's Angus animals (label `AN`) and Florida animals in the lowest ancestry decile, over the usable markers. **Pearson r ≥ 0.90 is required.** A lower value means the A/B assignments disagree between manifests, the dosages are meaningless, and the arm is reported as a harmonisation failure — not run, and no endpoint computed. This mirrors the Seminole-steer gate that preceded 6C₁ (which reached r = 0.993 against a threshold of 0.80).
- **Duplicate check.** Any Decker animal whose genomic correlation with a Florida animal exceeds 0.95 is excluded and counted.
- Scoring, ancestry estimation and support assessment run in the **shared-marker subspace**, with the support region refitted on Florida training animals restricted to those markers (Amendment 4). Mean-filling is not used.

## Breed classification, fixed before any score

The published breed codes are abbreviations; Table S1 of the manuscript, which defines them, is **not in hand**. Classification is therefore deliberately minimal, and uncertainty is resolved by exclusion rather than by guessing:

- **REFERENCE-ADJACENT (excluded from all endpoints):** `AN` (Angus) and `BR` (Brahman), the two breeds the Florida reference is built from, plus any breed label a reader of Table S1 would identify as an Angus- or Brahman-derived composite. These are excluded because whether the rule *should* accept them is not obvious — a purebred Angus sits at the edge of a composite herd's support region, and the Mizzou far-domain arm already refuses purebred taurine animals correctly.
- **OUT-OF-REFERENCE (the endpoint population):** every other breed label.
- **Any label whose meaning is uncertain stays in OUT-OF-REFERENCE** only if its ancestry coordinate places it in the endpoint set below; the endpoint is defined by a measured quantity, not by our reading of an abbreviation.

**Before unblinding, the abbreviation mapping must be confirmed against Table S1 of Decker et al. 2014.** If it cannot be obtained, the arm still runs — the primary endpoint does not depend on breed identity — and the classification-dependent exclusions are reported as provisional.

## Endpoints

### E1 (primary) — the C3 estimand, as a conditional rule

Among **out-of-reference animals whose estimated ancestry coordinate q_AB_axis falls inside the Florida training range** (0.035–0.915, so the min–max ancestry rule accepts them):

> **Pass if the k-NN support region refuses at least 90 % of them for ranking.**

Reported alongside: the share those animals are of the panel, and a breed-level bootstrap interval (2,000 replicates resampling breeds) so the figure is not driven by one large breed.

**Feasibility condition, fixed in advance:** at least **10 out-of-reference breeds** must have a median q_AB_axis inside the Florida range. Fewer than 10 and E1 is reported as inconclusive rather than passed or failed — the same structure that stopped 6C₁, where only one contemporary unit held both groups.

**What would falsify C3:** if those animals are largely *accepted* by the k-NN rule, then the mechanism C3 describes — an out-of-reference genome projecting into the middle of a two-reference axis while sitting outside the empirical support region — does not generalise beyond the 99 Holstein-Friesians it was found on.

### E2 (secondary) — refusal breadth

Overall ranking-refusal rate across all out-of-reference animals, and per breed. Expected near 1.0. This is descriptive of the layer's behaviour, not a test of C3.

### E3 (secondary) — ancestry-coordinate placement

The distribution of q_AB_axis across out-of-reference breeds, and the share of breeds whose median falls inside the Florida range. C3's mechanism predicts this share is **substantial, not negligible**: a two-reference estimator cannot express "neither Angus nor Brahman" and should place many foreign breeds mid-axis. A share near zero would mean the Holstein result was unusual rather than typical, which weakens C3's generality even if E1 passes on the few breeds available.

### Numeric prediction

Refused for every animal regardless of support, because no Decker animal comes from a Florida herd. Reported, not an endpoint.

## What cannot change

- No threshold, marker panel, prediction model or training set is modified by this arm, whichever way it falls.
- Nothing is tuned on Decker data. The support region is refitted on Florida **training** animals restricted to the shared markers, exactly as in the C3 diagnosis.
- One run. The prepare stage computes harmonisation and the QA gate; the unblind stage computes endpoints once, after verifying every hash in the lock.
- A failure is reported as a property of the applicability layer, and C3 would be downgraded accordingly.

## Prepare-stage outcome (16 September 2026): the gate failed, and the gate was wrong

Step 1 ran. Harmonisation: 42,889 of 43,043 Decker markers lift to ARS-UCD1.2, **2,891 match the panel by position, and all 2,891 carry a Florida A/B code**, so the usable count is 2,891 — above the 2,000 requirement and exactly at the point the coverage calibration measured. Genotype missingness 0.67 %. No Decker animal duplicates a Florida animal (highest correlation 0.42).

**The A/B consistency gate failed as pre-registered: r = 0.829 against a threshold of 0.90.** Under the protocol as written that ends the arm as a harmonisation failure.

The diagnosis says otherwise, and the gate is what is at fault:

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Markers with a frequency difference > 0.5 (the signature of a flipped A/B assignment) | **0 of 2,891** |
| Anti-diagonal share where Florida frequency is 0.8–1.0, the range where a flip is unmissable | **0.000** |
| Anti-diagonal share where Florida frequency is 0.4–0.6, where both diagonals coincide | 0.325 |
| Mean absolute frequency difference | 0.084 |

A flipped marker at Florida frequency 0.9 would appear near 0.1 in Decker. Not one marker behaves that way, and the apparent "anti-diagonal" markers sit at p ≈ 0.5, where the anti-diagonal *is* the diagonal. **There is no detectable A/B disagreement between the two manifests.**

The threshold was the error. It was borrowed from the Seminole-steer check (r = 0.993), which compared 1,052 animals **from the same ranch on the same array** against Florida. This gate compares **20** Decker Angus against a Florida group that is merely the most Angus-*like* decile of a composite herd (still up to 21 % Brahman). With 20 animals the binomial standard error alone is about 0.079 per marker at p = 0.5, and the F250 panel's frequency spectrum is ascertained and narrow, so a correlation of 0.90 is not attainable here even with perfect coding. I applied a number from one comparison to a structurally different one without checking it was reachable.

**The arm is halted.** It is not reported as a harmonisation failure, because that would state something the evidence contradicts; and it is not continued, because continuing past a failed pre-registered gate on my own judgement is exactly the move that makes a pre-registration worthless.

**What a corrected gate should be**, for a new pre-registration reviewed before use: a direct flip test rather than a correlation. For example — *the share of markers whose frequency difference exceeds 0.5 must be below 1 %, evaluated on markers where the Florida frequency is below 0.15 or above 0.85, where a flip is unambiguous and sampling noise cannot manufacture one.* That measures A/B agreement itself instead of measuring sample size and breed divergence. The present data would pass such a test, which is precisely why it must be specified and reviewed **before** it is adopted, not chosen because it passes.

## Amendment 1 — the A/B gate becomes a flip test (16 September 2026)

The correlation gate is replaced, on the project owner's decision after being shown the flip evidence above.

> **A/B consistency gate.** Among markers where the Florida counted-allele frequency is below 0.15 or above 0.85 — the range in which a manifest flip moves a frequency unambiguously — the share whose Decker frequency differs by more than 0.5 must be **below 1 %**, with at least **200** such markers available. The frequency correlation is still reported, as a descriptive number only.

This measures A/B agreement itself rather than sample size and breed divergence. The previous threshold could not be met by any correctly coded dataset of this shape, which is why it was replaced rather than relaxed.

**Disclosure, which travels with every result from this arm.** This gate was specified *after* seeing that the data pass it. That is the weakest part of this arm's provenance, and it is not disguised: a criterion chosen with knowledge of the outcome it produces is not equivalent to one fixed in advance. Three points bound the damage, and a reader may weigh them:

- the evidence is categorical rather than marginal — **0 of 2,891 markers** differ by more than 0.5, and 0.000 of markers in the unambiguous high-frequency band sit on the anti-diagonal, so the gate is not passing on a close call;
- the quantity being gated, whether two manifests agree on A/B, is a **property of the genotype coding**, independent of every endpoint this arm reports;
- **no endpoint quantity was computed** when this change was made: no ancestry coordinate, no support score, no acceptance decision existed for any Decker animal.

The endpoints, tolerances and the conditional rule in E1 are unchanged from the draft written before any Decker genotype was read.
